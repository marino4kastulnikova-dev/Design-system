## Behaviour

Figma describes this page as a purely visual composition. What the portal does, all <Proposed />:

- Pressing a quick action puts its text into the composer.
- The counter shows the typed length against the limit (drawn as "0/300").
- Send calls `onSend` with the trimmed text and clears the composer. Answers are not designed, so the portal shows none.
