## Anatomy

A frame with no children: height `borderWidth/hairline` (1 px), width Fill, filled with a border colour token. It is not interactive and has no states.

## Variants

| appearance | Token | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `default` | `border/default` | Rate block line in the Convert widget, table header bottom line |
| `inverse` | `border/inverse` | Sidebar footer line. White 4%: visible only on a dark surface |

Thickness is 1 px only. Length follows the container. Vertical dividers are not present in the product and are not created.

## Accessibility

From Figma: decorative. It carries no information and should be ignored by assistive technology; use `role="separator"` only where a real content separation exists.

## Edge cases and decisions

| Status in Figma | Finding |

| Out of scope | 4 empty "spacer" frames in the account cards are not dividers. |
