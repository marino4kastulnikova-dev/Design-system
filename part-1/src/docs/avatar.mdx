## Anatomy

A 56 × 56 circle (`size/avatar/lg`, `radius/full`) filled with `surface/inverse-subtle`, with centred initials in `typography/heading/h3` and `text/inverse`.

**Rule:** made for dark surfaces only. White text on a faint fill is invisible on a light background.

## Variants and sizes

One appearance and one size (56 px). A smaller size for tables is not needed: no avatar appears there. Image avatars are not present in the product and are not created. Not interactive: the surrounding profile row is.

Evidence: one occurrence, the profile block in the sidebar footer.

## Content

Initials are 1–2 letters. Figma also shows one-letter and three-letter content states.

## Accessibility

From Figma: the initials are decorative next to the user's name; the full name is the accessible text. White on the sidebar has high contrast. The portal renders the avatar with `aria-hidden`.
