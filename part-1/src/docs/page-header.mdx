## Composition

Organism. A single component: page title on the left, status and theme toggle on the right.

| Nested component | Instances | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LiveIndicator | 1 | Right side |
| ThemeToggle | 1 | Right side |

Evidence in Figma: the dashboard ("Welcome, Emily Gibson!").

The first block of the main panel.

The Users screen has a title "My Users" with a subtitle and no toggle. Figma marks this NEEDS CONFIRMATION: it is not yet a variant of this component, and the portal does not invent one.
